# Al Remal Restaurant Digital Ordering System

## Case Study

### Project Title

**Al Remal Restaurant Digital Ordering System**

### Client

**Al Remal Restaurant – Gaza, Palestine**

### Challenge

Al Remal Restaurant relied on traditional methods for displaying menu items and receiving customer orders, which made the ordering process slower and increased the possibility of communication errors between customers, cashiers, and kitchen staff.

The restaurant needed a digital solution that would allow customers to browse the menu, view prices, and place orders directly from their devices in a simple and organized way.

### Approach

To address this challenge, I designed and developed a web-based digital menu and ordering system tailored to the restaurant's workflow.

The frontend was developed using **Vue.js** to provide a fast, responsive, and user-friendly experience. Customers can browse food categories, view menu items and prices, select quantities, and submit their orders electronically.

On the backend, **PHP** was used to process and manage incoming orders. Once a customer submits an order, it is instantly delivered to the cashier's dashboard for review and processing. Additionally, the cashier can forward the order directly to the kitchen through WhatsApp with a single click, helping streamline communication between departments.

### Technologies Used

* Vue.js
* PHP
* HTML5
* CSS3
* JavaScript

### Results

* Digitized the restaurant's menu and ordering process.
* Reduced manual communication during order handling.
* Improved the customer experience through a modern ordering interface.
* Enabled cashiers to receive and manage orders efficiently.
* Simplified communication with kitchen staff through WhatsApp integration.
* Delivered a responsive solution that works across desktop and mobile devices.
